How to play

Tap, click or press Space to flap through the gaps. Each gate scores 1.

Touch an obstacle or the ground and the run ends.

At first, every 10 gates is a new stage, and each stage sets 5 banana friends free!

Golden coins are banana credits for the Upgrades shop.

Fly through the middle of a gap for a PERFECT. Skim an edge for a Close call.

Chimp's Navy, the water bonus after stage 4

Seen from above. Drag, move the mouse or use the arrow keys to steer.

Tap or press Space to LEAP over subs and barrels. No steering in the air!

Steer around seagulls: no leaping them.

Whirlpools slow you down.

You have 3 hearts. If the Navy gets you, you try just that part again.

Pop bubbles to free friends. A heart buoy half way wins back a heart.

Good to know

The pause button or the P key holds the game still.

Controller: any button flaps, Start pauses, the d-pad moves through menus (A picks, B goes back).

The last stage is Prince Chimp's jungle kingdom. Beat it to keep your upgrades or start fresh.
