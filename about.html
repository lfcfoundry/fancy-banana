What it is and how it works

Guide Fancy, a small flying banana in a hero mask and scarf, through the gaps of a side-scrolling course. One tap, click or Space press flaps. The whole game is one HTML file in plain canvas and JavaScript, with no libraries and no image files; all the art is drawn by code when the game starts.

Why it exists

A game anyone can open anywhere: one tap and no sound, so it works where sound is not welcome, with nothing to install and nothing leaving your device. Comfort is built in, and some of its code is shared as modules in JS-modules.

Highlights

One tap

Tap, click or press Space. Fixed physics at 120 steps a second.

Easy to learn

A ? button on the title screen opens a picture-by-picture How to play. No forced tutorial: tap start whenever you like.

Made to be comfortable

Calm mode, game speed down to 60%, left-handed layout, no flashing.

Stages and a water level

Candy Shop, Jam Jar Pantry, Ice Cream Parlor, Silverware Drawer, Chimp's Navy, the Fruit Garden and Prince Chimp's Jungle Kingdom.

Quiet by design

No sound or music at all. Everything is seen and touched: bright, readable pictures and a one-tap answer. Less to take in at once.

Free and private

No ads, accounts or purchases, and nothing sent anywhere. Progress is saved in your browser.

Free, private and open

Made for players of every age, with nothing hidden: what the game does is what you see.

No ads

Nothing to watch or skip. The game has no libraries and makes no network requests, so there is nowhere for an ad to come from.

No accounts

No sign-in and no profile. Progress is saved in your own browser; Save gives you a code or file to keep or move.

No data sent anywhere

Zero network requests: none were made during 15 seconds of play when checked in Chromium. Nothing leaves your device.

No microtransactions

Banana credits are earned by playing (friends freed plus coins). Nothing in the game is for sale.

Fully open source

MIT license. The readable source is in src/ in this repository, with the steps to rebuild the game.

Works offline

Once the page has opened, or after you save the file, it needs no connection. There is no background download or offline install, so the first open needs a connection unless you already have the file.

Single player

One player on one device. No online play, chat or leaderboards, and nothing to unlock with other people.

Easy on any modern browser

Chromium, Firefox and WebKitGTK are tested for every release. The game fits screens from 320 x 568 up to 2560 x 1440 in all three, and it needs no install.

A calmer way to play

The game has no sound and no music, ever, so there are no sudden sounds and nothing depends on hearing. Every cue is something you see, and the whole game is played by touch with one tap, a click or the Space key. That leaves less to take in at once, which can be easier on players who find noise and busy screens tiring.

Calm mode

Turns off flashes, shake and zoom. It switches on by itself if your device asks for less motion.

Slower speeds

Game speed from 100% down to 60%, and wider gaps in the first stages.

Large buttons and text

Bigger buttons, bigger text and a left-handed layout, all in Settings.

Your pace

A pause button (or the P key), a battery saver and game controller support.

The course

Every ten gates is a new stage, and each stage clear sets five banana friends free. Beat the whole Jungle Kingdom and the game starts to hide things: secrets, challenges, costumes and a few surprises for anyone who keeps playing.

Stage 1Candy ShopCandy canes, lollipops and gumball machines. The first two stages have wider gaps and a slower pace.
Stage 2Jam Jar PantryJars on shelves, a bowl of fruit and a copper pot.
Stage 3Ice Cream ParlorStacked scoops, cherries and a checkered floor.
Stage 4Silverware DrawerForks and spoons for gates, deep inside a dark drawer.
BonusChimp’s NavyA water level seen from above: steer and leap over submarines and barrels.
Stage 5Fruit GardenStarts on a beach and turns into the jungle island.
FinaleJungle KingdomPrince Chimp is waiting at the end.

Upgrades

Spend banana credits (friends freed plus coins) in the shop. There are 15 upgrades with 30 levels in all, costing 1,635 credits to get every one. These are the real shop tiles from the game, with the full wording underneath. A few of them:

Roomy gaps

Wider openings, +5 px per level.

3 levels, 10 to 30 credits
Gentle fall

Fancy falls 6% slower per level.

3 levels, 10 to 30 credits
Free bounce

Bounce off the ground or an obstacle and fly on, once per run per level.

2 levels, 25 and 50 credits
Banana magnet

Gold bananas fly to Fancy from farther away, +35% reach per level.

3 levels, 15 to 45 credits
Sweet spot

A wider PERFECT zone in the middle of every gap, +25% per level.

3 levels, 20 to 60 credits
Potassium Power!

Hold when three or more gates line up and Fancy glides straight through, a bit faster.

1 level, 120 credits
Second wind

Once per run, a crash restarts the stage you are in instead of ending the run.

1 level, 100 credits
